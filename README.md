## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Project Name: HarvestHub – A Smart Digital Marketplace for Farmers and Buyers

1. Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution

HarvestHub is a digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers and bulk buyers. The main purpose of this platform is to help farmers sell their products at better prices and help consumers purchase fresh agricultural products at affordable prices.

Our platform will have three main sections:

a. Farmer Section:

Farmers can create an account and upload details about their agricultural products.

They can mention the crop name, available quantity, expected harvest date and selling price.

Farmers can view different buyer offers and choose the price that suits them.

Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) can also use the platform to manage products from multiple farmers.

b. Buyer Section:

Consumers, retailers, hotels and other businesses can browse available agricultural products.

Buyers can compare prices and product details before placing an order.

They can directly contact farmers through the platform.

They can track their orders and receive delivery updates.

c. Transportation Section:

Local vehicle owners and transport providers can register on the platform.

Farmers and buyers can request transportation for their orders.

The platform can connect nearby farmers who are sending products to the same destination.

This allows farmers to share transportation costs instead of hiring separate vehicles.

HarvestHub will also use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to study previous market data and predict the demand for agricultural products. This can help farmers understand market trends and plan their sales in advance.

2. How It Addresses the Problem

In the traditional agricultural supply chain, products often pass through several intermediaries before reaching consumers. Every intermediary adds extra charges, which can reduce the profit received by farmers and increase the final price paid by customers.

HarvestHub aims to reduce this problem by creating a direct connection between farmers and buyers.

Farmers can display their products and prices on the platform without depending completely on middlemen. Buyers can compare available products and purchase directly from farmers.

The shared transportation facility will help reduce delivery expenses by combining products from nearby farmers into a single delivery trip.

This approach can improve price transparency, reduce unnecessary costs and make the agricultural selling process easier for both farmers and buyers.

3. Innovation and Uniqueness of the Solution

HarvestHub includes three important features that make our solution different:

a. Shared Transportation System

Small-scale farmers may not have enough products to fill an entire vehicle. Instead of arranging separate vehicles, HarvestHub will group orders from nearby farmers going to the same destination. This helps them share transportation expenses and reduces unnecessary vehicle trips.

b. Advance Crop Booking

Buyers can show interest in purchasing crops even before the expected harvest date. Using previous market demand and price information, the platform can suggest suitable buyers for upcoming harvests. This gives farmers an opportunity to plan their sales before harvesting.

c. Low-Internet Support

Some farmers may not have access to a stable internet connection. To solve this issue, HarvestHub will provide basic SMS-based notifications for important activities such as order confirmation, price updates and delivery information.


## Technical Approach
The following technologies can be used to develop HarvestHub:

Frontend: React Native or Flutter for developing a mobile application.

Web Development: React.js and Tailwind CSS for creating a simple and user-friendly website.

Backend: Python FastAPI or Node.js for handling user accounts, product listings and orders.

Database: PostgreSQL for storing farmer details, buyer information, products and transactions.

AI and Machine Learning: Python and Scikit-learn for studying market data and predicting product demand.

Route Optimization: Google OR-Tools for finding efficient transportation routes.

Maps: OpenStreetMap for showing locations and delivery routes.

Cloud Platform: AWS for hosting the application and storing data.

## Feasibility and Viability
1. Analysis of the Feasibility of the Idea

HarvestHub is practical because it uses technologies that are already available. Farmers can access the platform through smartphones, while FPOs can help farmers who are not familiar with digital applications.

The project can initially be introduced in a small agricultural market and later expanded to other regions.

The application does not require expensive equipment. It mainly depends on a mobile application, internet connectivity, a database and local transportation services.

Therefore, the project can be developed and tested using available technologies and resources.

2. Potential Challenges and Risks

1. Lack of Trust: Some farmers may hesitate to use a new digital platform because they are already familiar with traditional selling methods.

2. Product Quality: The quality of agricultural products shown online may sometimes differ from the actual products delivered.

3. Transportation Delays: Delays during transportation can affect the quality of fresh vegetables, fruits and other perishable products.

4. Poor Internet Connectivity: Farmers in some rural areas may face difficulties accessing online services because of unstable internet connections.

3. Strategies for Overcoming These Challenges

Building Trust: Work with local FPOs and agricultural groups to introduce the platform and help farmers understand its benefits.

Quality Verification: Introduce a basic quality-checking process at local collection centres before products are delivered.

Better Transportation: Partner with reliable local transport providers and provide regular delivery updates.

Offline Support: Provide SMS-based notifications for important activities so that farmers can receive updates even with limited internet access.

2. Benefits of the Solution

Social Benefits:

HarvestHub supports small-scale farmers and encourages the use of digital technology in rural communities. It also helps strengthen the role of FPOs in agricultural development.

Economic Benefits:

The platform can reduce unnecessary costs in the agricultural supply chain. Farmers get better opportunities to earn from their products, while consumers can benefit from more competitive prices.

Environmental Benefits:

Shared transportation and better route planning can reduce unnecessary vehicle travel. Faster product delivery can also help reduce food wastage, especially for perishable agricultural products.
